# Privacy Policy for Clockscape

**Effective date: September 20, 2026**

Clockscape is an ambient clock application developed and published by DevHarbor.

## 1. Information We Collect

Clockscape is designed to work without requiring an account or collecting personal information.

The app does not intentionally collect, sell, or share personal information such as your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, or other personally identifiable information.

Clockscape does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its core features.

## 2. Information Stored on Your Device

Clockscape may store app preferences and settings locally on your device so that your selected clock style, appearance, and other preferences can be retained.

This information remains on your device and is not transmitted to the developer.

## 3. Internet and Third-Party Services

Clockscape does not require an online account or server connection for its core clock functionality.

The app does not intentionally transmit personal information to the developer or third parties.

If a future version of Clockscape introduces third-party services or changes how user data is handled, this Privacy Policy will be updated before those changes apply.

## 4. Advertising

Clockscape does not currently display advertisements.

## 5. Data Security

Because Clockscape does not intentionally collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal information maintained by the developer to secure, sell, or disclose.

Local app preferences are stored on the user's device and are subject to the security protections provided by the Android operating system and the user's device.

## 6. Data Retention and Deletion

Clockscape does not maintain personal user data on developer-controlled servers.

You can remove locally stored Clockscape data by using Android's app data controls or by uninstalling the application, subject to the behavior of your Android device.

## 7. Children's Privacy

Clockscape does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users.

## 8. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated if Clockscape's functionality or data practices change.

Any updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date.

## 9. Contact

For privacy-related questions or concerns regarding Clockscape, please use the developer contact information provided on the Clockscape Google Play Store listing.
